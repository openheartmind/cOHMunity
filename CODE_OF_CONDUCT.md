# OHM Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in Open Heart & Mind (OHM).

We welcome participation regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, sexual identity or sexual orientation.

We recognise that diversity strengthens our community. We are committed to creating spaces where people feel safe, respected and able to participate openly, and where differences can be approached with curiosity, empathy and care.

## Our Standards

We expect all members of the OHM community to contribute to a positive, respectful and inclusive environment.

Examples of behaviour that contribute to this environment include:

* Demonstrating empathy, kindness and respect towards others.
* Respecting differing opinions, perspectives and experiences.
* Giving and receiving constructive feedback with openness.
* Taking responsibility for our actions, acknowledging mistakes and learning from them.
* Being mindful of how our words and actions affect others.
* Supporting the wellbeing of the community and contributing to the common good.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks or deliberate intimidation.
* Harassment in public or private spaces, including unwanted contact or persistent behaviour after being asked to stop.
* Publishing or sharing another person’s private information without their explicit permission.
* Discrimination, bullying, threats, aggression or violence.
* Any other conduct that is reasonably likely to make someone feel unsafe, unwelcome or unable to participate in the community.

This list is not exhaustive. Behaviour may breach this Code even if it is not explicitly listed above.

## Scope

This Code applies to everyone participating in OHM activities, including members, organisers, volunteers, contributors and attendees.

It applies across all OHM community spaces and activities, including:

* OHM gatherings, events and festivals.
* Workshops, meetings and other organised activities.
* OHM’s online platforms, social media accounts and communication channels.
* Interactions with other participants when officially representing OHM, including through email, social media or as an appointed representative at an event.

It may also apply to conduct outside OHM activities where that conduct materially affects the safety, wellbeing or participation of the OHM community.

Participation in OHM activities and spaces is subject to this Code.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Everyone participating in OHM is responsible for helping maintain a respectful and inclusive environment.

OHM’s Conflict Resolution Team is responsible for receiving concerns, explaining available resolution options and supporting the process for addressing complaints.

The OHM Board retains responsibility for significant organisational decisions, including formal findings, sanctions and restrictions on participation. Where appropriate, independent mediators or reviewers may be appointed.

OHM reserves the right to take appropriate action in response to behaviour that breaches this Code. This may include:

* Asking someone to stop or modify their behaviour.
* Removing or restricting inappropriate content from OHM communication channels.
* Issuing a formal warning.
* Restricting participation in particular activities or spaces.
* Temporarily or permanently excluding someone from OHM activities.

Responses will be proportionate to the nature and seriousness of the conduct. The measures outlined below are possible responses, not mandatory sequential steps. OHM may take more serious action immediately where circumstances warrant it.

## Reporting and Resolution

Anyone who experiences or witnesses behaviour that may breach this Code is encouraged to raise their concerns with the OHM Conflict Resolution Team.

Concerns will be acknowledged and considered promptly and fairly, in accordance with OHM’s Conflict Resolution Policy.

The policy provides options for:

1. Initial contact: raising a concern, seeking support or informing OHM of an incident without necessarily pursuing a resolution process.
2. Informal resolution: addressing a concern constructively, potentially through direct or indirect communication, mediator support or a facilitated discussion.
3. Formal resolution: formally documenting and assessing a complaint, gathering relevant information and determining appropriate action.

Informal resolution is not required before making a formal complaint. It may not be appropriate in situations involving serious misconduct, significant safety concerns or circumstances where a person does not feel comfortable participating.

OHM may take immediate protective action or refer matters to external authorities where necessary, particularly where there are serious concerns about safety or potential legal obligations.

Further information about the process is available in the OHM Conflict Resolution Policy.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on the circumstances, OHM may take one or more of the following actions.

### 1. Correction

For minor or isolated incidents, a private written or verbal communication may be used to clarify the behaviour that is inconsistent with this Code and explain the expected standard of conduct.

An apology or other corrective action may be requested where appropriate.

### 2. Warning

A formal warning may be issued where behaviour is more serious, has continued after correction, or otherwise warrants a formal response.

The warning will explain the behaviour that is unacceptable and the potential consequences of further violations. It may include conditions on future participation or interactions.

### 3. Temporary Ban

A temporary ban or restriction may be imposed in response to serious violations or sustained inappropriate behaviour.

This may involve restricting someone’s participation in OHM activities, access to OHM spaces or communication channels, or contact with particular individuals for a specified period.

Breaching these restrictions may result in permanent exclusion.

### 4. Permanent Ban

Permanent exclusion may be imposed where there is a pattern of serious or repeated violations, or a single incident of sufficiently serious conduct.

This may include harassment, threats, violence, sexual misconduct or other behaviour that poses a significant risk to the safety or wellbeing of the community.

## Protective Measures

OHM may implement temporary protective measures where necessary to safeguard individuals or the wider community while a concern is being addressed.

These measures may include temporary participation restrictions, arrangements to prevent contact between individuals or other steps considered appropriate to manage potential risks.

Protective measures do not, in themselves, constitute a finding that the Code has been breached. They will be proportionate to the circumstances and reviewed as appropriate.

## Confidentiality

OHM will respect the privacy of everyone involved in a concern or complaint.

Information will be shared only where necessary to assess or address the concern, protect the safety and wellbeing of the community, or meet legal obligations.

OHM will take reasonable steps to protect the confidentiality and security of information provided by people raising concerns and those responding to them.
